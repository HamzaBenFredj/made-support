MADE: Mafia 1926

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 October 2026

MADE is a single-player story game for iPhone. This policy explains, plainly, what the game does with your data.

The short version: MADE does not collect, transmit, or share any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising.

What we collect

Nothing. The game makes no network requests and contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking code. The developer cannot see how you play or anything about you.

Where your progress lives

Your save game (story choices, mission progress, cash and respect) and your settings (volume, haptics, text speed) are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They never leave it. If you use iCloud device backup in iOS Settings, iOS includes that storage in its backup like any app's data; the game itself never contacts iCloud.

Purchases

Version 1.0 has no in-app purchases. If a later version adds the paid unlock for Acts 2 to 5, Apple's App Store handles the payment; the game would only ask Apple's on-device StoreKit whether the unlock is owned. No payment or identity information would reach the developer.

Children's privacy

MADE does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
