Common questions

Where is my progress saved? On your iPhone only. The game autosaves when a mission starts, at checkpoints and when a mission ends. "Continue" on the title screen resumes from the last checkpoint.

A mission is too hard. When you fail, "Retry" puts you back at the last checkpoint with full health. The dodge roll makes you briefly untouchable; use cover and let the auto-aim pick the target.

How do I start over? "New Game" on the title screen starts Act 1 from the beginning and replaces your save.

Is there more? This is Act 1, "Errand Boy". Act 2, "The Water Run", is coming in an update.

Does the game collect data? No. See the Privacy Policy.
